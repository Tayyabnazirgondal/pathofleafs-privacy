# Privacy Policy for Path of Leafs

**Effective Date:** 15 August 2026  
**Last Updated:** 15 August 2026

Daynix Studio ("we", "us", "our") built the Path of Leafs app as a free 
mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app does and 
does not handle.

## Short Version

Path of Leafs does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal 
information. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

## What We Collect

**Nothing.** We do not collect any personal data from you.

The app has no user accounts, no login, no email collection, no 
analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

## What Is Stored On Your Device

The game saves your progress locally on your device using Android's 
built-in preferences storage. This includes:

- Level completion and star ratings
- Best times per level
- Daily streak counter
- Achievement unlock status
- Your settings (sound, music, vibration, theme)

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or anyone else.

If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted from your device by 
Android. You can also reset it manually from the in-app Settings screen.

## Internet Access

The app does **not** require an internet connection to work. All 30 
levels and all features are fully offline.

## Permissions

The app requests only one permission:

- **Vibration** — used to provide haptic feedback during gameplay 
  (e.g. when drawing a path, completing a level). You can disable this 
  in the app's Settings.

The app does **not** access your camera, microphone, contacts, 
location, storage, files, calendar, or any other personal data.

## Children's Privacy

The app is safe for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal 
information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect data from 
children under 13.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services that collect information.

## Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, the new version will be posted at the 
same URL with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the 
app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
